About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Copenhagen & the Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence in Copenhagen, Denmark. My current research focus is on Multilingual LLM post-training, NLP applications, and the influence of AI on society.

Previously, I did a Postdoc at Aalborg University, where I worked on NLP and Education. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the IT University of Copenhagen, defending my thesis, “Computational Job Market Analysis with Natural Language Processing,” in March 2024. I am still very much interested in these topics, so please reach out!
